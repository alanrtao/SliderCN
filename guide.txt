# 如何通关《滑块世界》

《滑块世界》并不简单！更确切地说，它不是一个适合一口气通关的游戏。在开发期间，我发现许多帮我试玩的朋友在卡关时会逐渐失去思路和耐心，并开始一遍遍重复同样或相似的解法。有句格言说：“只有疯子才会重复做同一件事而指望不同的成果”——也许我认识许多疯子吧！在卡关的时候，不妨休息一下，甚至等上一两天。这样断断续续的游戏体验会更加轻松愉快。

一般来说，每张地图最难的谜题是滑块3和滑块6。粗略的说，一个关卡的难度等于“机制交互的数量”乘以“活动空间的大小”——刚刚解锁的地图上没有太多的机制，而快要被填满的地图上则没有活动空间，所以进度中段的谜题是最难的。当然，我们不想让每一关千篇一律，所以它们的难度“曲线”是相似但不相同的。

如果这里的文字和截图不够直观，在油管上有本游戏的通关录像(感谢玩家@dogedeaf)!录像的置顶评论有每关对应的时间码：`<a href="https://youtu.be/BI7BdPrJBok" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://youtu.be/BI7BdPrJBok</a>`

# 石头镇

石头镇是本游戏的第一关。它会帮你认清《滑块世界》的基本原理。对了，这张地图是我们在GMTK游戏开发大赛上仅用48小时做出来的！

## 滑块二

游戏在“你”（探险家）的家里开始。在捡起古遗物之后，你发现屋外的世界很不对劲。你需要在探索地图来找到第二片滑块。

提示： ||尝试移动滑块并四处走动。不用担心，滑块的四边并不是空气墙！||

谜底： ||站在起始滑块上并将它移动到地图九宫格的右上角。你可以在滑块上方，地图边缘的一片空地上找到[滑块二]——还有一头牛。||

## 滑块三

收集[滑块二]之后，你可以开始在滑块之间走动并探索更多地图边缘的区域。[滑块三]可以很容易地通过[滑块二]来解锁。

谜底： ||将[带着房子的滑块]放到[河流滑块]左边，然后与遗迹边上的[考古学家]对话，她会给你[滑块三]。||

## 滑块四

你解锁了3片滑块，现在是第一关真正开始变难的时候了！想办法走到三面封闭的[滑块三]上，那是解锁下一片滑块的关键。

提示一: ||不要让你的思路被“拘束”！||

提示二: ||记得你是在哪里解锁[滑块二]的么？就在那头牛牛边上。||

谜底： ||从[带着房子的滑块]开始。站在上面并将它移动到中间行最右边。向右走到地图边缘的沙滩上，并捡起[一杯咖啡]。回到滑块上，并将它移动到地图右上。走到地图边缘，并把[三面封闭的滑块]移动到地图右上——这样你就可以走上滑块了。||

## 滑块五

你需要帮[滑块四]上的[渔夫凯文]解开缠住的浮标。你可以把它们捡起来然后放到任意地方。

谜底： ||你需要保证浮标之间的线段没有交叉。有一种解法是：事先把所有浮标移到河的同一边，然后一个接一个移到另外一边——直到不再有交叉。你需要把合适的滑块放到[河流滑块]的边上，这样才可以自由地过河。||

## 滑块六

新解锁的[滑块五]上面又有一座房子，里面有只猫猫——真不错！你需要帮屋外的[朱丽叶]找到她失踪的爱人。

谜底： ||要让有情人重逢，你需要将[带男人的滑块]（也就是带房子的初始滑块）移动到[带女人的滑块]边上（也就是带L形房子的滑块五）。||

## 滑块七

新解锁的[滑块六]上面有很多东西。[渔夫皮埃尔]需要鱼，背景里有个瀑布，还有一部分废墟。

提示一: ||渔夫需要鱼，而地图边缘海里的鱼想要游到地图左上方的瀑布那里。不过，现在还没有足够的“河流”滑块可以把两边连起来。你需要先解锁“另外的东西”才能够拼齐河流。||

提示二: ||把[带瀑布的滑块六]放到[带“直线河流”的滑块二]边上，你会发现滑块上方的遗迹图案是连起来的。||

谜底第一部分： ||你需要找到4片遗迹图案并将它们对齐在一起！仔细观察地图，可以发现有4个滑块的边角上各带着一个“圆形”图案：[瀑布滑块]的右上角、[水平河流滑块]的左上角、[起始房子滑块]的左下角、还有[“半岛”形状滑块]的右下角。||

谜底第二部分： ||把4个角对齐后，会出现一个“魔法箭头”。顺着这个箭头的指向，你可以走到[滑块五]的L形房子下面，那里有一块发光的石头。与石头互动来解锁下一片滑块。||

## 滑块八

你解锁了最后一片河流滑块！

提示： ||你已经集齐了所有4片河流滑块。是时候将它们拼齐并完成[渔夫]和[鱼]的任务了！||

谜底： ||依照以下顺序拼出瀑布到大海的河道：[瀑布滑块（6）]在地图左上，[水平河流滑块（2）]在最上行中央，[带浮标的拐角河流滑块（4）]在右上，最后剩下的[拐角河流滑块(7)]在中间行最右边。拼齐后，在地图边缘右方与[鱼]对话，之后再与[渔夫]对话以解锁[滑块八]。||

## 滑块九

你已经解锁了8片滑块！再想要将它们任意排列的话，想必是十分麻烦了。

### 大碴

[大碴]拿走了一块遗迹，那个王八蛋跳到了一块大石头上面，你没办法轻易拿走他的石板。你需要想办法让他“下来”。

谜底： ||移动[大碴]脚下的滑块会让他从石头上摔下来。这时你就可以捡起遗迹，把它交给[考古学家]了（她在带“水平河流”的[滑块二]上）。||

### 重排九宫

把遗迹碎片交给[考古学家]之后，她读出上面的铭文，使得地图随机排列了！你需要将地图重新拼齐。

提示： ||这是很经典的“重排九宫”（又称八数字推盘）游戏。对不熟悉的玩家来说，它的一大难点就是时常需要把已经归位的滑块挪开，好给其他滑块腾出过道来。有一种较为直接的解法是：先把一整行/列滑块归位；拼齐之后，这一行/列就不再需要移动了——你可以放心把它们放在一边，然后去解开剩下5片滑块的排列。||

谜底： ||哈哈，祝你好运 :) `<a href="https://www.wikihow.com/Solve-Slide-Puzzles" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://www.wikihow.com/Solve-Slide-Puzzles</a>`||

## 通关！

恭喜，你打通了石头镇这一关！你需要从进入地图中央的[村长家]，捡起[地图]，然后从地图右上方进入下一关——水帘洞。

# 水帘洞

欢迎来到水帘洞！洞里有很多真菌！
（其实原版文本里写的是Chloro Cave，主创在原文这段解释说他起这个名字的原因是叶绿素——chlorophyll——但是这和真菌好像并没有太大联系。译者取用“水帘洞”主要是因为当时正赶上《黑悟空》发布，其实也没什么道理。）

## 滑块二

你从南边进入了水帘洞。这张地图上已经有一片滑块被解锁了。在这片起始滑块上，你可以遇到一大丛“真菌”，里面藏着一片未收集的滑块。这个洞穴还藏着什么宝物呢？

提示： ||你可以通过起始滑块来探索很大一部分的地图边缘。多走一走，也许能找到些什么！||

谜底： ||从起始滑块开始，你可以找到地图边缘左下方的[扳手]和左上方的[剪线钳]。把它们交给地图边缘右上方的[工人]，他会把大灯泡修好，然后给你[滑块二].||

## 滑块三

真菌是会和灯光“交互”的。解锁[滑块三]并不需要太多步骤。

谜底： ||Move the tile you just got to the top right or bottom right corner of the grid. This will place it in the range of a light. The light will cause the fungi around the Slider to recede, allowing you to pick up the third Slider.||


## 滑块四


My god, another light! The fungi surely stand no chance against your electrical might now…


提示： ||Remember the Slider trapped in the fungi on tile 1?||


谜底： ||Place the first tile in the top-right corner, exposing it to light from the right. Place the third tile underneath the first tile, exposing it to light from the bottom. This will remove enough fungi for you to grab 滑块四 from the first tile.||


## 滑块五


On this tile is the cavern station. The power's gone out, and it's so dark that you can't even see the door!


谜底： ||Move the tile with the light underneath slider 4. This will allow you to enter the station. Upon entering the station, talk to Chad and you will receive 滑块五.||


## 滑块六


Here you'll find another light, though the cable has been chewed up by rats. The Archeologist makes her triumphant return, but she seems to have lost yet another item! What a klutz!


提示： ||You'll need to use the light on this tile in order to reach the flashlight, which is located on the slider with the big bridge.||


谜底第一部分： ||Pick up the key collectible from 滑块五, and return it to the worker on the left inside the cavern station. This will allow you to switch on power, powering the light on 滑块五 if it is to the left of the cavern station. You can also see the power line on the map, which lights up yellow once powered.||


谜底第二部分： ||Find the tile with the flashlight (the second slider you got, with the river and large bridge), and place lights above and to the left. That will allow you to access the flashlight in the fungi patch. Return it to the archeologist and pick up 滑块六.||


## 滑块七


This tile is dominated by a massive field of fungi, with 滑块七 deep inside. You'll have to make full use of what you've learned so far to solve the puzzle, but it is possible! Make sure you've turned on the Station light too.


提示一: ||This puzzle requires multiple moves, keep that in mind when setting up your grid.||


提示二: ||There is no arrangement of tiles that will light up a full path from the outside of the fungus to the next slider.||


提示三: ||You can move tiles around even while standing inside the fungus patch (assuming you're on a lit up space).||




谜底： ||Place the power station light in the center of the grid, the other light tile in the top left, and 滑块六 in the top middle. Enter the fungi field, and stand in the row where there is no rock on the right side. Slide 滑块六 to the right, and then move the single light tile underneath 滑块六. You can then pick up 滑块七.||




## 滑块八


What's this? A rat has stolen 滑块八, and is now running away! Time for your best Tom and Jerry impression. 


提示： ||The rat is faster, but you're smarter. Take advantage of the mechanics you've used so far, and your ability to move them.||


谜底： ||You can either corner the rat to catch it or chase it onto some removed fungi and then take away the light to regrow it. Either way, you now have 滑块八.||


## 滑块九


The archeologist has already made a great discovery on tile 8, but she can't quite read the runes. Light up the entire map! It doesn’t matter if the tiles are in the “correct” spots, so long as they are all lit up.


提示一: ||Try to avoid "double covering" tiles with multiple lights.||


提示二: ||You'll want a light in the center of the map.||


提示三: ||The other light should be in the top left or bottom left corner. Pay attention to where the walls are!||


谜底： ||There are multiple configurations that work. If you want some direction to work towards, the "correct" tile configuration is also a valid solution (the missing tile will be in the bottom-left). If you've completed the final puzzle in Stonybrook Village, this will be a breeze. When the caves are lit, a short cutscene will play, and then you'll receive 滑块九.||


## Area Complete!


Congratulations, you finished the Chloro Caves! On the final tile you'll find a gaggle of pirates, along with the archeologist and Chad. Pick up the boating license, because it's your ticket to the Shifting Seas. Head back to the Stonybrook Village pier, and get sailing!

# Shifting Seas


The Shifting Seas is Slider's water level. Like most water levels, there's a twist: instead of sliding tiles around, you rotate them. There's a lot to do and explore!


## 滑块二


Click on the arrows in the artifact in order to rotate tiles around.


谜底： ||Move onto the first tile, dock the boat, and walk over to the X. If you interact with the X, you'll dig Catbeard out of the sand, who will thank you and give you 滑块二.||


## 滑块三


With the tavern unlocked, you can meet Bob and some tavern goers. Bob won't respect you without an anchor though :(. There's a Slider in the corner, blocked by pirates playing a game of dice. If you can defeat them, it's all yours.


提示： ||You may have lost the initial roll, but you can still shake up the dice.|| 


谜底： ||After rolling, if you move the tile, it will increment the number on the dice. Move the tavern tile two times so that your total adds up to 11. You'll win the game and claim 滑块三||


## 滑块四


Finally, the Anchor, one of the most powerful artifacts in Slider! It allows you to pin down a tile.


谜底： ||In order to grab the Anchor, you need to be in the water and move adjacent to it. Then you can pick it up with 'E' (or whatever key action is bound to) and store it with 'Q' (or whatever key cycle is bound to). Once you have the anchor, return to Bob.||


## 滑块五


With the 4th slider unlocked, you can visit it and find the wizard Fezziwig. He's trying to cast a spell that moves tiles around, but he keeps falling off his rock.


提示： ||You have a tool that can pin down movement.||


谜底： ||Place the anchor on the tile Fezziwig is on in order to stop him from moving. You can then pick up his Magical Gem reward and turn it into Bob to upgrade your Tavern Pass.||


## The Open Seas


After you turn in Fezziwig's reward, Bob gives you the rest of the tiles in the area. How generous! Thus, you set off on a journey to plunder the seas of all their treasure. Each item can be turned into Bob in order to upgrade your Tavern Pass. You can finish it when you turn in all of them. 


You can ask Bob for hints in the game as well.


### Shipwreck


The shipwreck is the easiest of all the new puzzles.


谜底： ||You need to assemble the two pieces of the shipwreck on the map. If you investigate the area around it, you'll find a glowing spot you can interact with to dig up a Treasure Chest.||


### Buoys


Kevin is back on one of the beaches and asks for your help to untangle his buoys again.


谜底： ||It can help if you move the three main island pieces to the top-right in their respective spots. Otherwise just keep trying to move the buoys to the correct spots.||


### Volcano


The Volcano puzzle requires you to place the volcano in the middle, and surround it with the other tiles with rocks.


谜底： ||After placing the volcano tile in the middle, you can anchor it and rotate the other tiles until 3 of the rocks line up. You can tell they line up when the blue orb appears over them. For the last one, try anchoring one of the outer tiles instead of the volcano to finish adjusting.||


### The Foggy Seas


The Foggy Seas require you to travel the two foggy tiles in a specific order. You can get clues from several sources around the ocean.


提示一: ||The hints are given from Bob, the short child Ike in the tavern, and behind the tavern under the coconuts.||


提示二: ||The sequence is West, South, West, West, North, West.||


谜底： ||Start with both foggy tiles next to each other horizontally. Then, move from the right tile West, and it will light up one of the notes. Then, rotate the tiles so the other one is below yours, and move South onto it. The second note will be lit up. Continue this for West, West, North, West, until all 6 notes light up and reveal a hidden island.||


### Romeo and Juliette


Romeo and Juliette are seeking each other again. Romeo is on the top-left of the map trying to send a note to Juliette on the bottom-right, but it keeps sinking before it reaches her.


提示一: ||The bottle moves once whenever you move any tiles, and sinks after two moves. This means you have to get it to Juliette in two moves.||


提示二: ||The solution involves the Anchor.||


谜底： ||In order to move the bottle in two moves from the top-left to bottom-right, you need to make two diagonal moves. This can be done with the Anchor. If you put an anchor on the top-center or left-center tiles, you can rotate the tiles so that one of them makes a diagonal move. You can pick up the anchor and repeat the process to deliver the bottle with 2 diagonal moves.||


## Wrapping Up


After collecting all of the treasures from the seas, Bob asks you to put everything back to where it should be, one last time. You only need to place the three main island pieces in the top right (The one with the tavern, and the two beaches connected to it).


Then, Bob cuts down the trees blocking the way to Canopy Town.

# Canopy Town


Canopy Town features shape production and combination in a messy start up. You'll have to move Blobs so that they make and combine shapes to fulfill customers' orders.


## 滑块二+三


Welcome to the Jungle! To start off you have to learn how to use the huts, though they won't do much with only one.


谜底： ||Go upstairs using the ladder and talk to the person upstairs. He asks you to "Send some sticks up", which you can do by interacting with the signs on the Spawner until it has a stick symbol and an up arrow. Then, that guy tells you to go back down and report that the job has been completed.||


## 滑块四


It's time to start production. Barron asks you to give him a Stick and a Circle.


谜底： ||You can get a stick by sending a Stick into the hole. The Blobs will jump in the hole and you can pick their item up in the area downstairs, on the blanket by the big tree. To make a Circle, send two semi-circles to the same sign and they will combine.||


## 滑块五


Customers are asking for a Fish and a Boat. You'll have to find a configuration to combine the components to make them.

## 滑块六


It's time for a race. With Chad!


### Setting up the Race


There are a number of race cones representing the horizontal track between where Chad starts and the bell on the fat tile. To set up the track, place tile 5 to the left of tile 2/3. Before you can race though, Chad asks for a flag.


Flag 谜底： ||`<img src="Images/JungleFlag.jpg" class="img-responsive" alt="Flag Solution">`||


### Winning the Race


Now that the race is set up, you can start an attempt by talking to Chad while the race track is set up. But he's so fast! And if you break the track during the race, he stops it and calls you out for cheating.


提示一: ||Focus on the rules of the race -- what are the exact conditions for it starting or ending?||


提示二: ||You will have to use the Artifact to win.||


谜底： ||In order to beat Chad, you have to move faster than him. You can use the Artifact to move a tile, which moves faster than Chad. While you can't break the race track, you can move other tiles. To win, set up the race track and place a tile below your starting point. Start the race, hop onto the other tile, and move it all the way to the right. Then you can run up to the bell and ring it before Chad gets there.||


## 滑块七


Chad is defeated! You have picked up the Boots of Speed and the 6th tile with a duplicator on it. Now, we have three new orders for production as well: Crutches, a Bandage, and the Fish Bowl.


Crutches 提示： ||Some recipes require you to merge three shapes at once.||

## 滑块八

## How do Timed Gates Work?


This section is here to spell out how timed gates work, in case you have any confusion about them! Part of the fun is figuring them out and finding the edge cases you can exploit, but it's also spelled out here in case it gets frustrating.


The goal of the timed gates is to power all of its inputs within a certain number of moves. You can see the inputs that you need to power as batteries near the gate, or also the LED indicators on the actual gate itself. If you can power all of them, then the gate will stay on forever.


The large number on the gate represents how many tile moves you have left. If the gate has a 2 on it, then you only have two tile moves to power the entire gate. Conveyors do not count towards this limit!


The moves are only counted once they <i>completely</i> finish though. This means that you can chain together conveyor belt moves in order to squeeze more out of the very last moves.

# The Flats


The Flats region has you fighting, commanding your Robot army to defeat the apathetic Aliens. You get as many tries as you want to win this rock-paper-scissors area.


This area features a lot of randomness, so there is no explicit solution. However, we can try to give some tips.


- Try to win as often as you can (or tie if you can't). The first few waves of aliens will always go the same, but then it's up to you to maximize your potential.


- You don't have to use the supply reinforcements right away. Oftentimes it can be worth it to consider waiting until a better moment to create the troop.


- There are six waves total, with an increasing number of enemies. Additionally you will get a reinforcement roughly for every other Slider you pick up.


-The next wave will spawn once all the aliens in the current wave are defeated. Once there are 1-2 aliens left, try to rearrange the map and toops to prepare for the next wave.


-Try to keep the map as open as possible. It's easier to escape a bad situation by sliding 2-3 tiles away vs moving a flag. Place sliders with walls near the edge of the map.

# 额外成就


## 小面堡


Each area has a hidden Breadge to collect. They are all accessible even after beating the area. If you collect all 9, you can bring them to the bakery in the MRI for an awesome surprise. These are all possible to acquire even if you've collected all the tiles in an area. Good luck!


## Challenge Achievements

If you're still hungry for more challenges, each area has a bonus achievement. Most of these achievements require you to finish an area in a specific way, so you'll probably have to do it from a fresh save. Usually using cheats will disable these challenge achievements, but you can use the "Teleport to Area", "Spawn Anchor", and "Scroll + Boots" cheats to get to each of the areas for the achievement (*any cheat disables the speedrunning achievements on that save). There is also a one-hour speedrunning achievement that we think you'll enjoy a lot. Good luck figuring these out!
